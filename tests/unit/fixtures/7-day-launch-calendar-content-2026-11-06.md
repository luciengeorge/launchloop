## On-page intro (copy)

A day-by-day plan for the week before a launch. It covers Product Hunt, your share card, BetaList, Indie Hackers and a couple of free directories. Each task points back to a LaunchLoop guide or to the platform's own rules.

Print it, copy it into a note, or check the boxes as you go. Nothing here is a guarantee of traffic. It is a prep order so launch day is replies, not panic.

---

## Day 1: foundations (6 days out)

- **Pick the Product Hunt day.** Launches go live at 12:01 AM Pacific on the date you schedule. Product Hunt's help center says you can schedule any day within 30 days. Weekdays have more traffic and more competition; weekends are quieter on both counts. (Product Hunt launch checklist; How to launch on Product Hunt)
- **Make sure a stranger can use the product.** Sign up in a private window, on a phone, with a fresh email. Fix anything that blocks the first minute. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Put the product on your own domain.** BetaList's criteria list free hosting subdomains such as yourapp.vercel.app as a common pitfall. Its Help page: "Your startup must have its own domain." Do this before any BetaList submit. (How to launch on BetaList)
- **Decide whether Show HN fits.** Show HN wants something people can try now, not a landing page or waitlist. If it does not fit, skip it. (Show HN launch checklist)

## Day 2: the share card

When someone pastes your URL, the card comes from your og:image. Fix this before you submit anywhere.

- **Make one image at 1200x630.** Facebook asks for at least 1200 x 630. LinkedIn's minimum is 1200 x 627. X's archived docs want about 2:1 and under 5MB. 1200x630 is the compromise that fits all three. (OG image size)
- **Set `twitter:card` to `summary_large_image`.** Without it, X may show the small summary card. (OG image size)
- **If you change the image, change its URL too.** Facebook: use a new URL or the image will not update. (OG image size)
- **Test the live URL** with the free [OG preview](https://launchloop.dev/tools/og-preview). (OG image size)

## Day 3: Product Hunt listing draft

Write the listing now so launch day is replies, not drafting.

- **Name:** product name only.
- **Tagline: 60 characters max.** Say what it does and for whom. Test it with the [tagline checker](https://launchloop.dev/tools/product-hunt-tagline-checker). (Product Hunt launch checklist; How to write a Product Hunt tagline)
- **Description: 260 characters max.** Problem, what you do about it, one concrete detail only you can claim. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Topics:** up to three that match where your users browse.
- **Thumbnail 240x240. Gallery images 1270x760.** Lead with the result, not the login screen. Three to five images. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Draft the maker comment.** Why you built it, what it does today, what is next, and one specific question you want answered. You post this as soon as you go live. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Do not plan to ask for upvotes.** Product Hunt's Launch Guide: never ask outright for upvotes or run anything that requires them. Share the link and ask for feedback instead. (Product Hunt launch checklist; How to launch on Product Hunt)

## Day 4: BetaList and one free directory

- **Confirm BetaList still fits.** It wants unreleased or recently launched technology startups. It turns away free hosting subdomains, blogs, courses, consultancies and products that have already had significant press. (How to launch on BetaList; Product Hunt alternatives)
- **Know it is paid.** BetaList's Help page: "All submissions are paid. There is no free submission option." Paying buys a faster review, not a yes. The Submission Terms: "Priority listing guarantees faster review, not acceptance or any specific results." If it is not selected, Help says you get a full refund automatically. (How to launch on BetaList)
- **Write the fields within the limits.** Website URL 255, Name 255, Pitch 75 characters (one plain sentence), Description 700 characters plain text. The submission guide: visitors decide whether to click in about a second, so clarity beats cleverness. (How to launch on BetaList)
- **Be ready on any day.** The terms say you may be featured on any day including weekends and holidays. Do not promise your audience a date. (How to launch on BetaList)
- **Pick one free directory that fits, and read its rules.** From the Product Hunt alternatives post (checked on those sites' own pages in October 2026): Uneed has a free waiting line; DevHunt has a free listing for developer tools (a launch is paid); Peerlist Launchpad with a verified profile; Fazier's Basic tier (asks for a link back); Tiny Startups. Hacker News, Indie Hackers and Reddit list no fee for posting. Skip any site that does not match your product. (Product Hunt alternatives)

## Day 5: Indie Hackers (and other communities)

- **Create a product page and post a milestone.** Courtland Allen (2019): create a product page and post new milestones on a regular basis. (How to launch on Indie Hackers)
- **Write a forum post that teaches or asks, with the link at the end.** Allen's tips: share lessons and questions rather than announcements; leave your company name out of the title unless it is very well known; put the important part first. (How to launch on Indie Hackers)
- **Do not treat launch day as a spam pass.** Product Hunt warns: launch day is not a time to go spamming Reddit and Indie Hackers if you have not already been part of those communities. Warm up now, or skip. (How to launch on Product Hunt)
- **If you also post on Reddit,** follow that subreddit's own rules. r/indiehackers is not the same site as Indie Hackers. (How to launch on Indie Hackers; How to promote on Reddit)

## Day 6: prep day (1 day out)

- **Schedule the Product Hunt launch** for 12:01 AM Pacific tomorrow (or your chosen day). (How to launch on Product Hunt)
- **Finish the X thread and LinkedIn post** with the Product Hunt link, written ahead of time. Use a 1600x900 image for those posts if you have one; the site share card stays 1200x630. (Product Hunt launch checklist; OG image size)
- **Tell people the date without asking for votes.** Newsletter, early users, communities you already take part in. Ask them to check it out and send feedback. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **If BetaList is in flight,** keep tomorrow free enough to handle a feature on a weekend or holiday. (How to launch on BetaList)
- **Optional Show HN:** only if the product is ready to try. Title format and rules are in the Show HN checklist. Do not ask for upvotes or comments. (Show HN launch checklist)

## Day 7: launch day

- **Confirm the Product Hunt listing is live** just after 12:01 AM Pacific and post your maker comment. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Post the X thread and LinkedIn post** with the Product Hunt link. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Share the direct link.** Product Hunt says there is no penalty for linking directly to your product. Ask for feedback, never for upvotes. (Product Hunt launch checklist)
- **Reply to comments for the rest of the day.** Product Hunt's Launch Guide: show up and be present; reply quickly and honestly. (How to launch on Product Hunt)
- **If points drop,** in most cases Product Hunt's filters removed votes it judged non-genuine. Keep replying; do not chase votes. (How to launch on Product Hunt)
- **Indie Hackers forum post** can go out today or in the next day or two, as a lessons post, not a "we launched, upvote us" post. (How to launch on Indie Hackers)

## After the week

- Keep the Product Hunt product page updated.
- If you want a second BetaList feature, the criteria allow one pre-launch and one at launch with at least a few weeks between them. (How to launch on BetaList)
- For a Product Hunt relaunch later, Product Hunt asks for a significant update and at least two months between posts for the same product. (How to launch on Product Hunt)

## Honest limits (footer on the page)

This calendar is free prep, not a promise of visitors or signups. Platform rules change; when in doubt, read the live help page linked from each LaunchLoop guide. Every quote above was taken from those guides, which cite the platforms' own pages as of October 2026.
