## This week's tip: get your BetaList submission right

All from BetaList's own [Help page](https://betalist.com/help), [criteria](https://betalist.com/criteria) and [submission guide](https://betalist.com/help/submitting).

**1. It's paid now.** The Help page: "All submissions are paid. There is no free submission option." Most write-ups you'll find are from the free queue. Paying buys speed, not a yes. The [Submission Terms](https://betalist.com/terms/submissions): "Priority listing guarantees faster review, not acceptance or any specific results."

**2. Use your own domain.** The criteria list "Free hosting subdomains like yourapp.vercel.app" as a common pitfall.

**3. Write one plain sentence.** The pitch is 75 characters. The guide says "visitors decide whether to click in about a second, so clarity beats cleverness".

**4. Be ready on any day.** The terms say you "may be featured on any day including weekends and holidays." Flowrite was featured on a Saturday and [wrote](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/is-launching-on-betalist-worth-it-in-2021-here-are-our-numbers-3e65a95d5a) "it caught us with surprise".

**5. Keep expectations small.** Hashtag Slayer [paid $200](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/spending-200-to-earn-9-a7994aaab4) in 2020 to skip the wait. BetaList sent "222 unique visitors", 51 trial signups and two customers.

## And check your share card

When someone shares your site, the card comes from your og:image. Make it 1200x630.

Facebook says "Use images that are at least 1200 x 630 pixels". LinkedIn's minimum is "1200 (w) x 627 (h) pixels". X's archived docs want 2:1 and "less than 5MB", so 1200x630 is a fair compromise. Discord's doc only shows sizes in examples.

Set `twitter:card` to `summary_large_image`, or X may show the small card. If you change the image, change its URL too. You can test yours with my [OG preview](https://launchloop.dev/tools/og-preview).
