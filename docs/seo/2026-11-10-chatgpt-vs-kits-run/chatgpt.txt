2. Product description
Writing

Meet Zustand 🐻 — a small, fast, and scalable state management solution for React.

Tired of boilerplate, complicated abstractions, and wrapping your entire app in providers just to manage state?

Zustand makes state management refreshingly simple.

Create a store with a straightforward API, access it from any component using hooks, and update your state without unnecessary ceremony.

Why developers love Zustand:

⚡ Simple API: Create a store in just a few lines of code.
🪶 Minimal boilerplate: No providers, reducers, or complicated setup required.
🎯 Optimized rendering: Components subscribe to the state they actually need.
🔌 Flexible: Use middleware for persistence, Redux DevTools, Immer, and more.
🌐 Beyond React: Use vanilla stores without depending on React.
📦 Lightweight: A small library that doesn't get in your way.

Whether you're building a small side project or a complex application, Zustand gives you the tools to manage state without fighting your state manager.

Open source, MIT licensed, and built for the React community.

Give it a try, explore the docs, and help us make state management simpler for everyone!

🐻 GitHub: https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand
